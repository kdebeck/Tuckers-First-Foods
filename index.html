Stage 1 — Smooth + Simple

balanced order
This stage is arranged in a suggested balanced order, so you are not stuck doing all the iron-rich foods right away. Lower-allergen foods use 2 exposure circles.

Stage 2 — Thicker + Mixed

next step
Start once smooth purées are going well. Thicken familiar foods and combine ingredients Tucker already tolerates.

Stage 3 — Chunkier + Texture

skill-based
The goal is learning texture, not staying on purées forever. Move based on feeding skill rather than a strict age cutoff.

Major Allergens

slower + regular
Use the 3 circles below as early exposures for the major allergens. They do not have to be 3 consecutive days. Once tolerated, the more important thing is keeping them in regular rotation.

When to Increase Amount + Feedings

easy progression
Use this like a cheat sheet. Formula stays primary at first; the progression is gradual and based on interest.
Starting outOffer 1–2 teaspoons once daily. Let him eat more if he wants, but do not worry about a full serving at first.
Once the spoon is going betterGradually work toward roughly 1–2 tablespoons, then more if he clearly wants it.
When 1 meal feels easyAdd a second solids meal. A realistic rhythm is one meal at daycare and one after getting home.
As Tucker gets olderMove toward 2–3 meals per day, then 3 meals + snacks closer to age 1 as milk slowly becomes less dominant.
Example with your daycare schedule

~5:30–5:45 AM: bottle

6:30 AM: daycare drop-off

Daycare: bottles as usual; add a solids meal once he is ready for 2 meals/day

~4:40 PM: home

Right after getting home: purée/solids meal

~6:30–7:00 PM: last bottle

~7:30 PM: bedtime

Good stopping points: turning away, closing his mouth, pushing the spoon away, or losing interest.
